Why the tide varies at all

Sentinel-2 crosses Tauranga at the same local time every pass — about 10:20 am. The semidiurnal tide drifts against the five-day revisit, so a fixed morning slot sweeps the whole tidal range over a few weeks.

How the heights were derived

Each capture's exact acquisition instant was matched against LINZ's official Tauranga predictions and interpolated between the bracketing high and low. Tauranga is a standard port, so the tables are authoritative.

What this is not, yet

Crossfading photographs is a preview of the idea, not the shipping technique. The real build reduces each capture to a waterline and moves that line continuously — 9 photos become 9 calibrated contours over one fixed basemap.
