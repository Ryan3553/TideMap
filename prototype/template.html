What the damp band is

Freshly exposed flat is dark and wet; it pales as it dries. On an ebb a dark band follows the retreating water. On a flood there is none. Turn it off and try to tell which way the tide is going — that is the whole argument for keeping it.

Why real time looks still

The waterline advances a metre or two a minute at its fastest — about one pixel every ten minutes here. Light is what moves perceptibly. The tide is the slow truth underneath it.

Honest gaps

Below 0.33 m the drying map has no data, and never can: Sentinel-2's fixed morning overpass is phase-locked with the spring–neap cycle here, so spring low water is never observed. The piece breathes from 0.33 m up, then holds still at the very bottom. Water colour and sky are mine, not measured; the shape is real.
