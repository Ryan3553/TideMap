Tauranga Harbour, tide light

The contour ripples in the water are not decoration — they are lines of equal drying height, which is to say every waterline the harbour has had and will have. The bright one is now. At night the water keeps its own light and the towns come up.

The moon is why

Phase and altitude are computed for Tauranga, and the water's night glow follows them — brightest under a high full moon, nearly dark at new. Watch across a month and the spring tides arrive with the full and new moon, because they are the same fact.

What is real

Tide, sun, moon, the drying pattern and the built-up areas that light up. Colour is the one invented thing: the palette is a choice, applied to measured shapes.

Still to fix

Below 0.33 m the satellites never see, so the deepest channels hold still at spring low. Subtidal depth is inferred from image tone, not surveyed — the real bathymetry arrives with LINZ's coastal LiDAR.
