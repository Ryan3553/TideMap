Resolution: the basemap is Sentinel-2 at its native 10 m, and the drying-height data is ~15 m — that second number is the real ceiling on how sharp a waterline can be, and it is set by the pipeline, not the renderer. To go meaningfully finer we would swap the basemap for LINZ's 0.1 m aerial photography (free, open licence, needs an API key) and re-run the pipeline on a finer grid. Worth doing; not a one-line change.
